TREC Deep Learning Track passage judgements (qrels), produced by NIST
assessors and published at https://trec.nist.gov/data/deep2019.html and
https://trec.nist.gov/data/deep2020.html (retrieved 2026-09-23).

The judgement scale, quoted verbatim from the 2020 track data page:

  "Passages were judged on a four-point scale of Not Relevant (0),
   Related (1), Highly Relevant (2), and Perfect (3), where 'Related' is
   actually NOT Relevant---it means that the passage was on the same
   general topic, but did not answer the question."

That sentence is the reason this benchmark uses the source: the distinction
it draws is the one the task measures, and a person drew it.

The query text distributed alongside the judgements comes from MS MARCO and
carries the MS MARCO terms; see msmarco-TERMS.txt.
