First public cut of LLMSearchBench: 120 tasks, 8 models, and a documented
scoring protocol.

<!-- truncate -->

## Added

- **Task set v0.1.0** — 120 tasks across four categories (single-hop 30%,
  multi-hop 30%, freshness 20%, negative 20%), each admitted only after failing
  a no-tools memory check on three baseline models.
- **Four reported metrics** — accuracy, citation F1, unsupported-claim rate, and
  cost per 1 000 tasks. No composite score, on purpose.
- **Published run artefacts** — `raw.jsonl`, `summary.json`, and `manifest.json`
  per release, so every aggregate on the site can be recomputed.
- **This site** — versioned docs, a sortable results grid, and charts driven by
  the same `summary.json` the runner emits.

## Known limitations

- 120 tasks puts the accuracy noise floor around ±3 points. Differences under
  5 points are ties.
- Negative tasks are 20% of the set, which is enough to separate models that
  never refuse from models that sometimes do, but not enough to rank the
  latter against each other.
- Single-turn only. Nothing here measures long-horizon research behaviour.
