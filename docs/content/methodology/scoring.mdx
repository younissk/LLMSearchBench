# Scoring

Every metric below is computed per task, then averaged over the task set. No
metric is weighted into a single composite score — a composite hides the
trade-off between cost and quality, which is the interesting part.

## Accuracy

An answer is correct when a judge model marks it equivalent to the gold answer.
For a task set $T$ and a model $m$:

$$
\mathrm{Acc}(m) = \frac{1}{|T|} \sum_{t \in T} \mathbb{1}\!\left[\, \mathrm{judge}(a_{m,t},\, g_t) = \texttt{correct} \,\right]
$$

where $a_{m,t}$ is the model's answer and $g_t$ the gold answer. Negative tasks
count as correct when the model declines and says why.

## Citation F1

Let $C_{m,t}$ be the set of URLs the model cited and $S_t$ the gold source set.
Precision and recall are set-wise, and the reported figure is their harmonic
mean:

$$
P = \frac{|C_{m,t} \cap S_t|}{|C_{m,t}|}, \qquad
R = \frac{|C_{m,t} \cap S_t|}{|S_t|}, \qquad
F_1 = \frac{2PR}{P + R}
$$

Citing ten sources to catch the right two is penalised: that is what $P$ is for.

## Unsupported-claim rate

Answers are split into atomic claims $c \in \mathcal{C}(a_{m,t})$. A claim is
*supported* when a judge finds it entailed by at least one retrieved document:

$$
\mathrm{UCR}(m) = \frac{1}{|T|}\sum_{t \in T}
\frac{\bigl|\{\, c : \neg\,\mathrm{supported}(c) \,\}\bigr|}{|\mathcal{C}(a_{m,t})|}
$$


## Cost

Cost is billed tokens at list price, including tool-call overhead and the
retrieved documents fed back into context, scaled to 1 000 tasks:

$$
\mathrm{Cost}_{1k}(m) = \frac{1000}{|T|} \sum_{t \in T}
\left( p_{\text{in}} \cdot n^{\text{in}}_{m,t} + p_{\text{out}} \cdot n^{\text{out}}_{m,t} \right)
$$

Judge-model tokens are excluded — they are a property of the harness, not of
the model under test.

## Judge protocol

The judge is a fixed model pinned per release, prompted with the gold answer and
asked for a three-way verdict (`correct` / `incorrect` / `unjudgeable`).
Unjudgeable tasks are dropped from the denominator and logged; a release with
more than 2% unjudgeable is rejected and the tasks rewritten.

:::warning[Judge drift]
Changing the judge model changes every number. The judge is part of the release
identity — see [versioning](../versioning.md).
:::
